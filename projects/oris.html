ORIS

A family of three audio products with one design language, where the headphones harvest light through a flexible solar cell in the headband.

Year
2024
Context
Year 1, Linköping University
Products
S1 compact speaker, S2 home speaker, S3 headphones
Capabilities
Industrial design, CMF, CAD, rendering

Introduction

Three products, one design language: calm, soft volumes broken by precise horizontal grooves.

The brief was to develop a technical product with clear requirements on production, users and design, then extend it into a product family.

S1

Compact speaker. Shares its organic, pebble-like form with the headphones.

S3

Headphones with a solar cell in the headband.

S2

Home speaker. Shares the horizontal grooves with S1.

Materials

Cool, exact metal against soft, tactile textile.

Materials chosen to echo the calm of nature: metal frames the sound and gives a precise feel, while mesh textile adds warmth where the products meet the body.

Energy

Headphones that charge from the light around them.

A thin, flexible printed solar cell from Epishine, a Linköping company, follows the curve of the S3 headband and tops up the battery whenever the headphones are worn or left in a lit room. The cell becomes part of the look instead of being hidden.

Material highlights

Recycled aluminium

Ear cups and stand in recycled aluminium, which takes around 5 % of the energy of new aluminium and can be recycled again and again.

Recycled mesh textile

Headband and ear cushions in a mesh woven from recycled PET. The cushions are removable, so they can be washed or replaced.

Printed solar cell

Epishine's organic cell is printed in a thin roll-to-roll process and replaces part of the battery the headphones would otherwise need.

Use phase

Light tops up the battery, so the headphones are charged less often, and the battery works through fewer cycles over its life.

End of life

Cushions, headband and battery can be replaced instead of the whole product, and the aluminium can be recycled.
