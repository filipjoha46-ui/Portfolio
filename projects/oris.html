ORIS

A family of three audio products with one design language, where the headphones harvest light through a flexible solar cell in the headband.

Year
2024
Context
Year 1, Linköping University
Products
S1 compact speaker, S2 home speaker, S3 headphones
Capabilities
Industrial design, CMF, CAD, rendering

Introduction

One family, one language.

The brief was to develop a technical product with clear requirements on production, users and design, then extend it into a product family that proves a coherent design language.

ORIS is a simple, Scandinavian family: the compact S1 speaker, the larger S2 home speaker and the S3 headphones. Calm, soft volumes are broken by precise horizontal grooves, which tie the two speakers together, while S1 and S3 share the same organic, pebble-like forms.

Materials

Metal and textile in balance.

The material choices are meant to echo the calm of nature. Metal gives the products a precise, elegant feel and frames the sound, while a soft mesh textile on the headband and ear cushions adds warmth. Together they create a clear contrast: cool and exact against soft and tactile.

  • MetalPolished shells and grooved bodies for an exact, premium expression.
  • Mesh textileSoft, breathable surfaces where the products meet the body.
  • Horizontal groovesOne shared detail that reads across the whole family.
  • Soft volumesRounded, organic forms that feel calm on a desk or a shelf.

Energy

Headphones that charge from the light around you.

The headband of S3 integrates a solar cell from Epishine, a Linköping company that prints thin, flexible organic solar cells made to harvest energy from everyday indoor light. Because the cell is flexible and only fractions of a millimetre thick, it can follow the curve of the headband instead of sitting on top as a separate panel.

The concept: the headphones top up their battery whenever they are worn or left on the stand in a lit room, so they need charging less often. The cell also becomes part of the look, a fine pattern of cells in the headband that shows the technology instead of hiding it.
