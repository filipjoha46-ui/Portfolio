About

Linköping, Sweden

I'm a fourth-year M.Sc. student in Design and Product Development who likes to work where the sketch meets the workshop.

I combine digital visualisation with hands-on prototyping, most recently in my bachelor's thesis with Reijmyre Glasbruk, where I designed and built the Nikolina pendant lamp. Before that I spent over two years planning pallet transport across the Nordics, which taught me to think about manufacturing, logistics and real-world use, not only form.

I tutor industrial design at Linköping University, teaching concept development, sketching, prototyping and 3D tools. In 2026 I'm on exchange at National Taiwan University's Department of Mechanical Engineering.
