What I bring

I design products that look right, work right and can actually be built.

01

Concept to working prototype

I take ideas from sketch to CAD to a physical, working object, whether that means CNC-turned aluminium, 3D-printed parts or workshop models.

02

Design for production

I think about how parts are made, mounted and assembled: standard components, tolerances and solutions that survive the real world, like hand-blown glass that is never the same twice.

03

Visualisation

I communicate ideas with renders, animations and presentation material in Blender and Unreal Engine, so decisions can be made early.

04

Structured and hands-on

Two years of transport planning across the Nordics taught me to plan, prioritise and deliver. I lead teams, organise events for 150 guests and tutor industrial design at Linköping University.
